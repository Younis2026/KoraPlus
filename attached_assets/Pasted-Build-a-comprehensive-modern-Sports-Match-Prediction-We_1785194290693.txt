Build a comprehensive, modern Sports & Match Prediction Web Application styled for mobile and desktop screens, completely localized in Arabic (RTL support). 

The app should feature a bottom navigation bar (or responsive header) with 5 main sections:

1. Home (الرئيسية):
   - Top breaking news banner.
   - Today's matches carousel/section.
   - Live matches indicator.
   - Top leagues/tournaments quick links.
   - User's upcoming predictions summary.
   - Current user rank and points widget.
   - Top 3 leaderboard highlight of the week.
   - Latest news feed tailored to the user's favorite team.

2. Matches (المباريات):
   - Filters: Today's Matches, Live Matches, Tomorrow's Matches, Past Results, and By League.
   - Match Detail View (when clicking a match):
     * Starting Lineups.
     * Missing/Injured Players.
     * In-depth Match Statistics.
     * Minute-by-minute Live Commentary / Match Events timeline.
     * Goalscorers, Cards, and Substitutions tabs.
     * Fans' prediction poll results.

3. Predictions & Gamification (التوقعات - Core Feature):
   - Available matches to predict on (Score prediction, First Goalscorer, Man of the Match, Total Goals count).
   - Dynamic crowd prediction percentage and potential points calculator.
   - Sub-tabs/Pages:
     * My Predictions (توقعاتي).
     * Prediction Results & History (نتائج التوقعات).
     * Global & Friends Leaderboard (ترتيب المستخدمين).
     * Weekly & Monthly Rewards/Prizes showcase (الجوائز الأسبوعية والشهرية).

4. News & Media (الأخبار):
   - Categories: Breaking News, Transfers, Player Injuries, Press Conferences, Articles & Analyses, Video Highlights & Goals.
   - Filter news by favorite club/league.

5. User Profile (حسابي):
   - Profile picture, User Name, Total Points earned, Global Rank, and Badges/Achievements.
   - Favorite Teams & Favorite Leagues manager.
   - History of all past predictions and scores.
   - App Settings & Technical Support/Feedback page.
   - Logout option.

Design Requirements:
- Modern, clean UI (dark mode preferred for sports apps).
- Fully responsive layout with smooth transitions.
- Populate with realistic mock data in Arabic for matches, news, and leaderboards so the app feels completely alive out-of-the-box.
